## Design direction

- Quiet personal utility rather than a generic AI dashboard.
- Android/Pixel-native proportions and interaction patterns.
- Warm neutral surfaces with restrained blue accents.
- Mostly flat layers; avoid excessive gradients, glass, glow, and decorative AI effects.
- Smaller radii and tighter hierarchy than the earlier concept.
- Real product specificity: actual projects, travel, language practice, notifications, builds, recipes, memory, and routines.
- Task-first navigation: Today / Chat / Learn / Inbox / More.
- Model names (Scout/Muse/Sage) are secondary implementation details, not primary navigation.
- Production states must include offline, permission denied, not installed, loading, error, success, and partial-data states.
